# Company Classification, Tiers, and Hiring Schedules

## 1. Purpose
This document provides a detailed breakdown of partner recruiting companies, their tier classification, compensation packages, baseline eligibility metrics, and evaluation round structures for the placement season at Vasavi College of Engineering.

## 3. Detailed Selection Round Framework
### 3.1 Online Assessment (OA) Regulations
- **Duration & Supervision:** OA tests range from 90 to 120 minutes with mandatory webcam and screen proctoring.
- **Cutoff Criteria:** Candidates must score above the 75th percentile in both section-wise aptitude and automated coding test cases to qualify for technical rounds.

### 3.2 Technical Interview Requirements
- **DSA & Problem Solving:** Candidates are tested on data structures (Trees, Graphs, DP) and algorithm efficiency (Time/Space Complexity).
- **System Design & Architecture:** Dream Tier technical rounds include live code walkthroughs on HackerRank or CoderPad platforms, alongside low-level system design problems.

### 3.3 HR and Behavioral Evaluations
- **Communication & Fit:** Assesses background verification, projects documentation, soft skills, and willingness to work at specified company locations.
- **Mandatory Documents:** Candidates must present original physical grade sheets and identity proof at the time of HR interviews.
