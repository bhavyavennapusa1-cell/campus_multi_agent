# Campus Placement Eligibility and Recruitment Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes the eligibility criteria, company categorization tiers, offer lock rules, and branch exceptions governing campus recruitment and placement drives conducted by the Training & Placement (T&P) Cell at Vasavi College of Engineering.

## 2. Company Categorization and Criteria
Campus recruiters are classified into three distinct categories based on compensation packages (CTC) and technical domain:

### 2.1 Dream Tier (CTC >= 18.0 LPA)
- **Minimum CGPA:** 8.0 cumulative GPA with zero active backlogs.
- **Academic Gap:** Maximum 1 year gap allowed between 12th grade and undergraduate admission.
- **Participation Limit:** One-time placement privilege per student (once placed in a Dream company, no further placement participation is permitted).

### 2.2 Core Engineering Tier (CTC 7.0 LPA to 17.9 LPA)
- **Minimum CGPA:** 7.0 cumulative GPA.
- **Backlog Ceiling:** Maximum 1 active backlog permitted at the time of recruitment drive registration.

### 2.3 Mass / Pool Tier (CTC 3.5 LPA to 6.9 LPA)
- **Minimum CGPA:** 6.0 cumulative GPA.
- **Backlog Ceiling:** Maximum 2 active backlogs permitted at the time of drive registration.

## 3. The One Offer Lock Rule
### 3.1 Primary Offer Binding
Once a student receives a formal job offer or pre-placement offer (PPO) from a participating company, they are immediately locked and barred from attending subsequent placement drives in that company tier or lower tiers.

### 3.2 Dream Upgrade Exemption
A student placed in a Mass Tier or Core Tier company is granted a one-time upgrade exemption to apply for Dream Tier drives, provided:
1. The student maintains a CGPA >= 8.5.
2. The Dream drive occurs within 60 calendar days of the initial offer letter date.

### 3.3 Offer Rejection Penalty
If a student accepts a company offer during a campus drive and subsequently declines or fails to join without valid medical justification 30 days prior to onboarding, the student will be permanently blacklisted from all T&P Cell alumni placement assistance services.

## 4. Branch-Wise Exceptions and Internships
### 4.1 Internship-to-FTE Conversion
Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs) generated from 6-month or full-year industry internships are considered binding final offers under §3.1.

### 4.2 Core Branch Provisions
Students in Mechanical, Civil, and EEE branches who accept a Mass Tier IT software offer retain a special provision allowing them to attend 1 additional Core Tier engineering drive relevant to their native branch.
