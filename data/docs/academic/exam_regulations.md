# End-Semester and Internal Examination Regulations

## 1. Purpose
This regulation outlines the evaluation scheme, grading scale, revaluation procedure, malpractice rules, and backlog reappear policy for all undergraduate engineering programs at Vasavi College of Engineering.

## 2. Grading Scale and Grade Point System
### 2.1 Letter Grades & Point Mapping
Academic performance is awarded using a 10-point letter grading scale:
- **O (Outstanding):** Marks >= 90.0% | Grade Point: 10
- **A+ (Excellent):** Marks 80.0% - 89.9% | Grade Point: 9
- **A (Very Good):** Marks 70.0% - 79.9% | Grade Point: 8
- **B+ (Good):** Marks 60.0% - 69.9% | Grade Point: 7
- **B (Above Average):** Marks 50.0% - 59.9% | Grade Point: 6
- **C (Average):** Marks 40.0% - 49.9% | Grade Point: 5
- **F (Fail):** Marks < 40.0% | Grade Point: 0

### 2.2 Evaluation Split
- **Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE):** Accounts for 40% of the total course marks (2 internal exams of 30 marks each + 10 marks assignments/quizzes).
- **Semester End Examination (SEE):** Accounts for 60% of the total course marks.

### 2.3 Passing Criteria
To pass a course, a student must secure:
1. A minimum of 40.0% marks in the SEE paper (minimum 24 out of 60 marks).
2. A overall aggregate of 40.0% marks combining CIE and SEE (minimum 40 out of 100 marks).

## 3. Revaluation and Recounting Process
### 3.1 Application Window
Students dissatisfied with their SEE marks must apply online through the examination portal within 7 calendar days from the date of official result declaration.

### 3.2 Fee Structure
- **Recounting Fee:** ₹300 per subject.
- **Revaluation Fee:** ₹1,000 per subject.
- **Answer Script Photocopy Fee:** ₹500 per subject.

### 3.3 Grade Revision Threshold
- If revaluation results in a mark variation >= 15.0% of the maximum marks, the paper undergoes a 2nd independent evaluation, and the average of the two closest marks is awarded.
- If mark variation is < 5.0%, the original grade remains unchanged.

## 4. Malpractice Categories and Disciplinary Action
### 4.1 Category 1 Malpractice
Carrying unauthorized notes, smartwatches, or mobile devices into the exam hall without active copying:
- **Action:** Cancellation of the current examination paper and a financial penalty of ₹1,000.

### 4.2 Category 2 Malpractice
Active copying, sharing materials, or impersonation:
- **Action:** Cancellation of all written exams of the current semester, 1-year academic suspension, and referral to the Disciplinary Committee.

## 5. Backlog and Reappear Rules
### 5.1 Supplementary Examinations
Supplementary examinations for failed courses are conducted within 30 calendar days following regular semester result declarations for final-year students, or during the annual semester break for other batches.

### 5.2 Maximum Attempt Limit
A student is permitted a maximum of 4 attempts to clear any backlog course. Failing all 4 attempts requires mandatory re-registration for the course during regular semester execution.
