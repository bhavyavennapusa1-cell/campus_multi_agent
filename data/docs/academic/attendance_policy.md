# Institutional Attendance Policy and Regulations

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to establish clear, objective, and enforceable guidelines regarding student attendance requirements at Vasavi College of Engineering. Regular class attendance is mandatory for academic progress and eligibility for end-semester assessments.

## 2. Minimum Attendance Requirements
### 2.1 Standard Threshold
Students must maintain a minimum of 75.0% aggregate attendance across all registered theory and laboratory courses in a given semester to be eligible to sit for the Semester End Examination (SEE).

### 2.2 Condonation Range (65.0% to 74.9%)
Attendance falling between 65.0% and 74.9% may be condoned strictly on valid medical grounds or official institution representation.
- **Medical Grounds:** The student must submit a registered medical practitioner's certificate within 3 calendar days of returning to campus.
- **Approval Workflow:** Requires recommendation by the Head of Department (HOD) and final approval by the Principal.
- **Condonation Fee:** A mandatory administrative condonation fee of ₹500 per subject must be paid upon approval.

### 2.3 Critical Shortage (<65.0%)
Students whose aggregate attendance falls below 65.0% are strictly detained. 
- Detained students will NOT be permitted to sit for the SEE or any makeup examinations under any circumstances.
- A detained student must repeat the entire semester in the subsequent academic year by paying the full term fees.

## 3. Calculation Methodology
### 3.1 Per-Subject vs. Overall Calculation
- Aggregate attendance is calculated as `(Total Period Conducted Attended / Total Periods Conducted) * 100`.
- In addition to the 75.0% aggregate requirement, a student must maintain a minimum of 70.0% attendance in each individual subject to appear for that specific subject exam.

### 3.2 Approved Institutional Absences
- Attendance credit (On-Duty / OD) for representing the college in sports, hackathons, or cultural events is capped at a maximum of 10.0% of total conducted classes.
- OD requests must be pre-sanctioned by the HOD at least 7 calendar days prior to the event date.

## 4. Appeal and Condonation Procedure
### 4.1 Submission Window
All attendance condonation applications and supporting documents must be submitted to the Academic Section strictly within 5 working days prior to the declared final attendance closing date.

### 4.2 Review Committee
Applications are evaluated by the Attendance Appeals Committee comprising the Dean of Academic Affairs, the respective HOD, and the Campus Medical Officer.

### 4.3 Decision Timelines & Finality
The committee decision will be published within 72 hours of submission. The decision of the Principal is final and binding; no further appeals will be entertained.
