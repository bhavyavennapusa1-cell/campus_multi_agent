# Open and Professional Elective Enrollment Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy regulates the selection, capacity allocation, prerequisite verification, and swap deadlines for Open Electives (OE) and Professional Electives (PE) across undergraduate departments at Vasavi College of Engineering.

## 2. Elective Selection and Allocation Mechanics
### 2.1 Capacity Limits
- Each elective section has a strict maximum capacity cap of 60 students.
- An elective course requires a minimum enrollment of 20 students to be operational during a semester.

### 2.2 Allocation Ratio
Elective seat allocation is executed dynamically via the Student Information System (SIS) using a dual-tier model:
- **50% Seat Quota (First-Come, First-Served):** Opened online at 09:00 AM on the designated enrollment day.
- **50% Seat Quota (Merit-Based):** Allocated strictly based on CGPA at the end of the preceding semester.

### 2.3 Prerequisite Verification
- Advanced electives require the student to have passed foundational prerequisite courses with a minimum grade of 'B' (6.0 grade points).
- The SIS automatically verifies prerequisite compliance before confirming registration.

## 3. Class Strength Limits and Course Cancellation
### 3.1 Minimum Threshold Cancellation
If total confirmed registration for an elective course remains below 20 students 3 calendar days prior to semester commencement:
- The course will be officially cancelled.
- Affected students will be notified via institutional email and provided a priority 48-hour window to opt into alternative running electives.

### 3.2 Parallel Section Creation
When registration demand for an elective exceeds 90 eligible applicants, the department may establish a second section, subject to faculty and room availability.

## 4. Swap and Withdrawal Deadlines
### 4.1 Elective Swap Window
Students are permitted to change or swap their registered elective strictly within the first 10 calendar days of the semester start date via the SIS portal.

### 4.2 Late Withdrawal Restrictions
- No elective course changes or withdrawals are allowed after 17:00 IST on the 14th calendar day of the semester.
- Students failing to attend un-swapped electives after 14 days will be marked absent, resulting in a zero score for internal assessments.
