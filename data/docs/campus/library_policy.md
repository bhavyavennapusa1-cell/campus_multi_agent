# Central Library Rules, Borrowing Limits, and Digital Access

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets forth the operating hours, borrowing privileges, overdue penalty structures, replacement procedures, and digital resource usage guidelines for the Central Library at Vasavi College of Engineering.

## 2. Membership and Borrowing Allowances
### 2.1 Undergraduate (UG) Student Allowances
- **Quota:** Maximum of 3 books at any given time.
- **Loan Period:** 14 calendar days from the date of issue.

### 2.2 Postgraduate (PG) & Research Scholar Allowances
- **Quota:** Maximum of 5 books at any given time.
- **Loan Period:** 21 calendar days from the date of issue.

## 3. Fine Structure and Renewal Guidelines
### 3.1 Overdue Fines
- Books retained beyond the due date incur an overdue fine of ₹2.00 per day per book for the first 7 days of delay.
- Starting on the 8th day overdue, the fine increases to ₹5.00 per day per book until returned.

### 3.2 Book Renewal Rules
- Issued books may be renewed for a maximum of 2 consecutive loan cycles.
- Renewal must be completed online or at the circulation desk prior to the due date.
- Renewals are disallowed if the book has been reserved by another member.

## 4. Lost Book Replacement Policy
### 4.1 Replacement Cost Structure
In the event of a lost or damaged library book, the borrower must comply with one of the following settlement options within 15 calendar days:
1. **Replacement with Book:** Supply an identical or newer edition of the lost title plus a non-refundable administrative processing fee of ₹150.
2. **Monetary Reimbursement:** Pay 150.0% of the current market or publisher catalog price of the book.

## 5. Library Hours and Digital Facilities
### 5.1 Facility Operating Hours
- **Working Days (Monday to Saturday):** 08:00 to 22:00 IST (Reading Room & Digital Section).
- **Sundays & Institutional Holidays:** 09:00 to 17:00 IST.

### 5.2 Digital Resource Usage
- 40 dedicated high-performance workstations are available in the Digital Library for accessing IEEE, Springer, and ScienceDirect journals.
- Computer usage is capped at a maximum of 120 minutes per student per day.
