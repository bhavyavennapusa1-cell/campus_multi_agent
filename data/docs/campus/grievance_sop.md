# Standard Operating Procedure for Campus Grievance Redressal

## 1. Purpose
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the workflow, categorization, resolution Service Level Agreements (SLAs), and escalation hierarchy for student and staff grievances at Vasavi College of Engineering.

## 2. Classification of Grievances
Grievances submitted to the institutional portal are classified into four distinct categories:
- **Academic Grievances:** Evaluation discrepancies, attendance logging errors, course coverage, or faculty conduct.
- **Hostel & Mess Grievances:** Food quality, hygiene, room maintenance, water/electricity utilities.
- **Infrastructure & Transport Grievances:** Laboratory equipment defects, bus schedule delays, classroom amenities.
- **Harassment & Anti-Ragging:** Physical, verbal, or digital abuse, ragging, or gender discrimination.

## 3. Submission Channels and Resolution SLAs
### 3.1 Submission Methods
Grievances must be registered electronically via the Student Grievance Portal (`grievance.vasavi.ac.in`) or submitted via official email to `grievances@vasavi.ac.in`.

### 3.2 Category-Wise Service Level Agreements (SLA)
- **Academic Grievances:** Mandatory resolution within 5 working days from submission.
- **Hostel & Infrastructure Grievances:** Mandatory resolution within 48 hours of ticket logging.
- **Harassment & Anti-Ragging Claims:** Immediate mandatory acknowledgment within 24 hours, followed by a formal inquiry committee hearing within 72 hours.

## 4. Redressal Machinery and Escalation Hierarchy
### 4.1 Level 1: Departmental / Hostel Warden Level
Initial review and remediation conducted by the respective HOD or Chief Warden.

### 4.2 Level 2: Institutional Grievance Redressal Committee (IGRC)
If a ticket remains unresolved past the specified SLA timeframe, it automatically escalates to Level 2 for review by the IGRC chaired by the Dean of Student Affairs.

### 4.3 Level 3: Principal & External Ombudsman
If the student is dissatisfied with Level 2 findings, a final appeal can be lodged within 7 calendar days to the Principal and AICTE-appointed External Ombudsman, whose decision within 14 working days is final.
