# Institutional Transport Routes, Schedules, and Pass Policy

## 1. Purpose
This document provides comprehensive information on official college bus routes, pickup schedules, fee payment guidelines, digital RFID pass policies, and pass replacement procedures for Vasavi College of Engineering.

## 2. Designated Bus Routes and Timings
The college operates a fleet of air-conditioned buses across major city corridors. All buses arrive on campus by 08:15 IST and depart at 16:30 IST on working days.

### 2.1 Route 12 (Kukatpally / Miyapur Corridor)
- **Coverage Areas:** Kukatpally Housing Board (KPHB) -> Miyapur X Road -> Hafeezpet -> Kondapur.
- **Morning Pickup:** KPHB 06:45 IST | Miyapur 07:00 IST | Campus Arrival: 08:15 IST.

### 2.2 Route 18 (Secunderabad / ECIL Corridor)
- **Coverage Areas:** ECIL X Road -> Malkajgiri -> Tarnaka -> Uppal X Road.
- **Morning Pickup:** ECIL 06:30 IST | Tarnaka 06:50 IST | Campus Arrival: 08:10 IST.

### 2.3 Route 24 (LB Nagar / Dilsukhnagar Corridor)
- **Coverage Areas:** LB Nagar Metro -> Dilsukhnagar -> Koti -> Mehdipatnam.
- **Morning Pickup:** LB Nagar 06:40 IST | Dilsukhnagar 06:55 IST | Campus Arrival: 08:15 IST.

### 2.4 Route 31 (Gachibowli / Hitec City Corridor)
- **Coverage Areas:** Hitec City Railway Station -> Cyber Towers -> Gachibowli ORR Junction.
- **Morning Pickup:** Hitec City 07:00 IST | Gachibowli 07:15 IST | Campus Arrival: 08:15 IST.

## 3. Bus Pass Application and Fee Structure
### 3.1 Annual Transportation Fee
- The transport fee is ₹32,000 per academic year, payable in 2 equal installments of ₹16,000 at the beginning of each semester.

### 3.2 RFID Pass Verification
- Students must tap their RFID Smart Bus Pass on the onboard validator upon entering the bus.
- Riding without a valid active RFID pass incurs an immediate penalty fine of ₹200 per ride.

## 4. Pass Replacement and Transport Office Contact
### 4.1 Lost Pass Replacement Procedure
In case of a lost or damaged transport RFID card:
1. File an application at the Transport Desk.
2. Pay a card re-issuance fee of ₹350 at the cash counter.
3. The replacement card will be issued within 48 hours.
