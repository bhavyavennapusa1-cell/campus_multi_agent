# Residence Hall and Hostel Regulations

## 1. Purpose
These rules govern student conduct, safety, curfew, outpass procedures, mess operations, and visitor access for all residential hostels at Vasavi College of Engineering.

## 2. Curfew Timings and Gate Control
### 2.1 Standard Curfew Hours
- **Weekdays (Monday to Friday):** Hostel main entry gate closes strictly at 22:30 IST (10:30 PM).
- **Weekends (Saturday and Sunday):** Hostel gate entry closes at 23:30 IST (11:30 PM).

### 2.2 Late Entry Penalty Framework
- **1st Offense:** Written warning logged in biometric record + automated SMS alert dispatched to parent/guardian.
- **2nd Offense:** Financial penalty of ₹500.
- **3rd Offense:** 7-day hostel suspension and mandatory parental meeting with Chief Warden.

## 3. Leave Application and Outpass Protocol
### 3.1 Advance Application Requirement
Outpass requests for overnight stay or home visits must be logged through the Student Hostel Portal at least 48 hours prior to the intended departure time.

### 3.2 Parental Verification
All night outpass approvals require mandatory parental authorization via an automated IVRS call response or SMS confirmation from the parent's registered mobile number.

## 4. Mess Operations and Meal Policy
### 4.1 Mess Service Schedule
- **Breakfast:** 07:30 to 09:00 IST
- **Lunch:** 12:15 to 14:00 IST
- **Evening Snacks:** 17:00 to 18:00 IST
- **Dinner:** 19:30 to 21:15 IST

### 4.2 Mess Rebate Rule
Residents absent from the hostel for a minimum of 5 continuous calendar days are eligible for a mess fee rebate at the rate of ₹75 per day.
- **Notice Period:** Rebate applications must be submitted on the portal at least 3 calendar days prior to commencement of absence.

## 5. Room Allotment and Visitor Rules
### 5.1 Allotment Criteria
- 1st and 2nd-year undergraduate students are allotted triple-sharing rooms.
- 3rd and 4th-year students are allotted double-sharing rooms, prioritized based on previous academic year CGPA.

### 5.2 Visitor Access Restrictions
- Visitors (parents/guardians only) are permitted exclusively in the Hostel Visitors Lounge between 16:00 and 19:00 IST.
- Day scholars, friends, and overnight guests are strictly prohibited inside resident rooms; violation incurs a fine of ₹2,000 per instance.
