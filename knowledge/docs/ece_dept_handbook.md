# Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) Department Handbook

## 1. Department Leadership & Laboratories
The Department of ECE is led by Dr. E. Sreenivasa Rao (FAC105). Laboratories include Digital Signal Processing Lab, VLSI Design Center (Cadence/Synopsys tools), Microprocessors Lab, and Embedded Systems Lab.
- HOD Office: Ramanujan Block, Room R201. Email: `hod.ece@vasavi.ac.in`.

## 2. Hardware Lab Safety & Equipment Issue Rules
- Component Issue: Oscilloscopes, FPGA trainer kits, and sensor kits are issued against student ID cards at the ECE store room (Room R205).
- Lab Safety: Breadboard and PCB soldering work must be conducted under technician supervision. Damaged ICs must be reported immediately.
