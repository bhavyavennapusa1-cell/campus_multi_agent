# Vasavi Placement Eligibility & Company Tier Regulations

## 1. Company Classification & Tiers
- Dream Tier (CTC >= 18.0 LPA): Minimum CGPA 8.0 with zero active backlogs. Attendance >= 75%.
- Core Tier (CTC 8.0 - 17.9 LPA): Minimum CGPA 7.0 with maximum 1 active backlog. Attendance >= 70%.
- Mass / Pool Tier (CTC < 8.0 LPA): Minimum CGPA 6.0 with maximum 2 active backlogs. Attendance >= 65%.

## 2. Multi-Offer and Upgrade Rules
- Primary Offer Binding: Once a student receives a formal job offer or pre-placement offer (PPO) from a company in Mass or Core Tier, they are locked into that offer and may ONLY apply for Dream Tier companies.
- Dream Upgrade Exemption: A student placed in a Mass Tier or Core Tier company is granted a one-time upgrade chance to participate in Dream Tier recruitment drives.

## 3. Placement Suspension Rules
- No-Show Penalty: Registering for a placement drive and failing to appear for the online assessment (OA) or interview without prior notice results in immediate suspension from the next 2 company drives.
