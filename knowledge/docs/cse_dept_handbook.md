# Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) Department Handbook

## 1. Department Overview & Administration
The Department of CSE is headed by Dr. T. Adilakshmi (FAC100). The department houses 12 specialized computer centers, an AI & Robotics Center of Excellence, and high-performance computing clusters.
- HOD Office: Vasonomics Building, Room V301. Email: `hod.cse@vasavi.ac.in`.

## 2. Lab Infrastructure & Operating Hours
- Computing Labs (Lab 1 to Lab 8): Equipped with 450+ High-Performance Intel i7/NVIDIA GPU Workstations running Ubuntu 24.04 LTS & Windows 11.
- Lab Hours: 08:00 AM to 07:00 PM on weekdays. Open access for capstone project development.

## 3. Mandatory Capstone Project Guidelines
3rd and 4th-year students must complete a major capstone project in teams of 2 to 4 students.
- Evaluation Stages: Phase-1 Review (End of Sem 6, 30% weightage), Phase-2 Final Defense & GitHub/Research Paper submission (End of Sem 8, 70% weightage).
- Plagiarism Threshold: Turnitin similarity index must be strictly below 15%.
