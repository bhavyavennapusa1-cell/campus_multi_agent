# Vasavi College of Engineering - Attendance Regulations

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to establish clear, objective, and enforceable guidelines regarding student attendance requirements at Vasavi College of Engineering. Regular class attendance is mandatory for academic progress and eligibility for end-semester assessments.

## 2. Minimum Attendance Criteria
1. Standard Requirement (>= 75.0%): Students must maintain a minimum of 75.0% aggregate attendance across all registered theory and laboratory courses in a semester to be eligible for end-semester examinations (SEE).
2. Condonation Range (65.0% to 74.9%): Attendance between 65.0% and 74.9% may be condoned by the Principal / Head of Department (HOD) strictly on valid medical grounds or official institution representation.
   - Medical Grounds: The student must submit a registered medical practitioner's certificate within 3 calendar days of returning to campus (or department office).
   - Approval Workflow: Requires recommendation by the Head of Department (HOD) and final approval by the Principal.
   - Condonation Fee: A mandatory administrative condonation fee of ₹500 per subject must be paid upon approval.
3. Critical Shortage (< 65.0%): Students with aggregate attendance below 65.0% are strictly detained, not eligible for condonation, and will NOT be allowed to sit for end-semester examinations (SEE) or makeup exams under any circumstances. Detained students must repeat the course/semester in a later academic year by paying full term fees.

## 3. Calculation Methodology & Per-Subject Rules
1. Per-Subject Threshold: In addition to the 75.0% aggregate requirement, a student must maintain a minimum of 70.0% attendance in each individual subject to appear for that specific subject exam.
2. Approved Institutional Absences (On-Duty / OD): Attendance credit (On-Duty / OD) for representing the college in sports, hackathons, or cultural events is capped at a maximum of 10.0% of total conducted classes (up to 10 working days). OD requests must be pre-sanctioned by the HOD at least 7 calendar days prior to the event date.

## 4. Medical Leave & Makeup Exam Rules
1. Medical Leave Exclusion: Medical leave with valid documentation may be excluded from attendance calculations up to a maximum of 10 working days per semester.
2. Makeup Exam Eligibility: A makeup exam request is valid ONLY if attendance is >= 65.0% AND absence was due to an officially approved medical emergency or institution-represented event.

## 5. Appeal and Condonation Procedure
1. Submission Window: All attendance condonation applications and supporting documents must be submitted to the Academic Section strictly within 5 working days prior to the declared final attendance closing date.
2. Review Committee: Applications are evaluated by the Attendance Appeals Committee comprising the Dean of Academic Affairs, the respective HOD, and the Campus Medical Officer. Decision is published within 72 hours. Principal's decision is final.
