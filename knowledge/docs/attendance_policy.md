# Attendance policy

Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible
to sit for the semester-end examination.

Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for condonation through
the department office, subject to approval by the Head of Department.

Students below 65% attendance are not eligible for condonation and must repeat
the course in a later semester.

Medical leave with valid documentation may be excluded from attendance
calculations, up to a maximum of 10 working days per semester.
