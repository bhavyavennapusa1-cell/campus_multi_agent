# Vasavi Campus Student Handbook & Code of Conduct

## 1. Campus Dress Code and Attendance Protocol
Students are expected to adhere to formal/semi-formal dress code during academic hours (09:00 AM to 04:30 PM). Wearing institutional identity cards (ID cards) is mandatory at all times on campus premises, library, labs, and cafeteria.

## 2. Code of Academic Integrity and Discipline
Plagiarism in lab assignments, capstone projects, or midterm exams results in immediate grade penalty and referral to the Academic Discipline Committee. Unfair means during semester examinations lead to subject cancellation and automatic backlog recording.

## 3. Anti-Ragging Strict Policy & Helpline
Vasavi College maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards ragging in any form. Ragging is a criminal offense punishable under UGC regulations. Immediate suspension and police lodging will follow any reported ragging incident.
- Anti-Ragging Helpline Contact: +91-9876599999
- Email: antiragging@vasavi.ac.in

## 4. Student Mentorship Program
Every student is assigned a faculty mentor for their 4-year academic duration. Mentors conduct bi-weekly check-ins to monitor CGPA progress, attendance alerts, career goals, and emotional well-being. HOD oversight occurs monthly.
