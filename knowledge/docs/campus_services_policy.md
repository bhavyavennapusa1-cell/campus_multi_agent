# Campus Transport, Health Center & Auxiliary Services

## 1. Campus Bus Fleet & Pass Registration
The college operates 25 AC/Non-AC buses connecting major twin-city corridors (Kukatpally, Dilsukhnagar, Secunderabad, Uppal).
- Bus Pass Registration: Annual bus fee INR 22,000 paid at transport cell in June/July.
- Bus Timings: Buses reach campus by 08:45 AM and depart at 04:45 PM. Evening extension bus departs at 06:15 PM for library/sports students.

## 2. Health Center Medical Facilities
Full-time resident medical officer and nursing staff available at the Health Center (Block-B Ground Floor).
- Emergency Care: 24/7 ambulance facility available on campus for hospital transfers.
- Medical Leave Certificate: Medical leave applications for class absence must be countersigned by the Resident Doctor within 3 days of returning to campus.

## 3. Cafeteria and Food Court Operations
Central Food Court operates from 08:00 AM to 07:00 PM serving multi-cuisine meals and snacks. Quality audits are conducted monthly by FSSAI certified inspectors and student mess representatives.
