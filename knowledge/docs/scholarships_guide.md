# Campus Scholarships & Student Financial Assistance

## 1. State Post-Matric Tuition Fee Reimbursement (EPass)
Government tuition fee reimbursement program for SC, ST, BC, and EBC category students.
- Eligibility: Annual parental income below INR 2.0 Lakhs (INR 2.5 Lakhs for SC/ST). Minimum 75% attendance required.
- Financial Support: Covers up to INR 35,000 per academic year.
- Application Deadline: October 15 every year via ePASS Telangana portal.

## 2. Institutional Merit-cum-Means Endowment Scholarships
Endowment fund awards for academically meritorious students facing financial hardship.
- Eligibility: Top 5% academic scorers in semester exams with CGPA >= 8.5 and family annual income below INR 5.0 Lakhs.
- Award Amount: INR 50,000 per annum credited directly to tuition accounts.
- Deadline: September 30 via Student Welfare Office.

## 3. AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girl Students
Government initiative to empower female students pursuing technical engineering education.
- Eligibility: Girl students in 1st or 2nd year B.Tech, annual family income <= INR 8.0 Lakhs. Up to 2 girls per family.
- Amount: INR 50,000 per annum towards tuition and computer equipment purchase.
- Deadline: November 01 on National Scholarship Portal (NSP).
