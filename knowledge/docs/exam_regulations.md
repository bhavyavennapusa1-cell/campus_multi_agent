# End-Semester and Internal Examination Regulations

## 1. Grading Scale and Point System
Academic performance is awarded using a 10-point letter grading scale:
- O (Outstanding): Marks >= 90.0% | Grade Point: 10
- A+ (Excellent): Marks 80.0% - 89.9% | Grade Point: 9
- A (Very Good): Marks 70.0% - 79.9% | Grade Point: 8
- B+ (Good): Marks 60.0% - 69.9% | Grade Point: 7
- B (Above Average): Marks 50.0% - 59.9% | Grade Point: 6
- C (Average): Marks 40.0% - 49.9% | Grade Point: 5
- F (Fail): Marks < 40.0% | Grade Point: 0

## 2. Passing Thresholds and Weightage
1. Combined Weightage: Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) accounts for 30%-40% of total grade (2 mid-term tests of 20-30 marks + quizzes/assignments); Semester End Examination (SEE) accounts for 60%-70%.
2. Passing Criteria: A student must score a minimum of 40% in the SEE paper (minimum 24/60 or 28/70) AND achieve an overall aggregate score of at least 40% (CIE + SEE) to clear a course.

## 3. Supplementary and Re-Evaluation Rules
1. Supplementary Exams: Held twice a year (after odd and even semester results) and within 30 days of results for final year students. Students with active backlogs may register for supplementary papers (max 4 attempts per course before mandatory re-registration).
2. Re-Evaluation & Recounting: Students may apply within 7 to 14 days of result publication. Recounting fee: ₹300 per subject; Revaluation fee: ₹1,000; Answer Script Photocopy: ₹500. Grade revision occurs if mark variation exceeds 10%-15%.

## 4. Malpractice Categories and Disciplinary Action
1. Category 1: Carrying unauthorized notes, smartwatches, or devices without active copying -> Cancellation of current paper + ₹1,000 fine.
2. Category 2: Active copying, sharing materials, or impersonation -> Cancellation of all written exams of the semester, 1-year academic suspension, and referral to Disciplinary Committee.
