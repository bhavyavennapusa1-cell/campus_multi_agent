# Central Library Regulations & Digital Access

## 1. Library Working Hours and Reading Rooms
The Central Library operates under the following timings:
- Regular Weekdays: 08:00 AM to 08:00 PM
- Saturdays: 09:00 AM to 05:00 PM
- Examination Periods: Extended reading room access from 08:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

## 2. Book Borrowing Limits and Renewal Rules
- Undergraduate Students (B.Tech): Up to 4 physical books for a period of 14 days.
- Postgraduate Students (M.Tech): Up to 6 physical books for a period of 21 days.
- Renewal: Books can be renewed online twice if not on reservation by another user.
- Overdue Fines: INR 5.00 per day per overdue book. Loss of book requires replacement with latest edition plus 20% processing fee.

## 3. Group Discussion Room Booking Guidelines
The library houses 4 dedicated Group Discussion (GD) rooms equipped with smart screens.
- Max Booking Duration: 2 hours per team session.
- Advance Booking: Applications via library portal up to 3 days in advance. Silence must be maintained in general corridors outside GD rooms.

## 4. Digital Subscriptions & Remote Access
Students have 24/7 off-campus proxy access to IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Springer Journals, and NPTEL course archives. Credentials are sent to official college emails at time of admission.
