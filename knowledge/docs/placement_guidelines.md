# Campus Placement Guidelines & Recruitment Policy

## 1. Company Tier Classification & Eligibility Criteria
Companies visiting campus for recruitment are classified into three distinct tiers:
- **Dream Tier (CTC >= 20 LPA)**: Minimum CGPA 8.0, 0 active backlogs, >= 75% attendance. Examples: Google, Microsoft, Salesforce, Uber.
- **Core Tier (CTC 8 - 19 LPA)**: Minimum CGPA 7.0, <= 1 active backlog, >= 70% attendance. Examples: Oracle, Cisco, Deloitte, L&T.
- **Mass / Pool Tier (CTC < 8 LPA)**: Minimum CGPA 6.0, <= 2 active backlogs, >= 65% attendance. Examples: TCS, Cognizant, Wipro, Infosys.

## 2. Multiple Placement Offer Policy & One-Student-One-Job Rule
- Basic Rule: A student securing an offer in a company of a lower tier remains eligible to apply for companies in higher tiers (e.g. Mass $\rightarrow$ Core $\rightarrow$ Dream).
- Dream Tier Lockdown: Once a student receives an offer from a Dream Tier company (>= 20 LPA), they are considered fully placed and excluded from subsequent drive registrations to allow remaining peers opportunities.

## 3. Placement Drive Registration & No-Show Penalties
Students must register for individual placement drives via the campus placement portal 48 hours prior to the online test date.
- No-Show Penalty: Registering for a company drive and failing to appear for the online test or technical interview without prior approved leave results in immediate suspension from the next 2 placement drives.

## 4. Internship to Full-Time Conversion (PPO Guidelines)
Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs) earned during 3rd-year summer internships must be reported to the Placement Cell within 7 days. PPOs above 20 LPA automatically count as Dream Tier offers.
