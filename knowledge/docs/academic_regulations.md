# Institutional Academic Regulations & Grading Policy

## 1. Credit Structure and Promotion Criteria
Undergraduate B.Tech programs require completion of 160 total credits across 8 semesters.
- Promotion from 1st Year to 2nd Year: Minimum 50% credits earned in 1st year subjects.
- Promotion from 2nd Year to 3rd Year: Minimum 60% credits earned in 1st and 2nd year subjects combined.
- Promotion from 3rd Year to 4th Year: Maximum 4 active backlogs allowed at registration time.

## 2. Attendance Shortage Condonation and Detention Rules
- Minimum required aggregate attendance: 75.0%.
- Condonation eligibility: Attendance between 65.0% and 74.9% may be condoned by the Principal on valid medical grounds with a doctor's certificate and payment of INR 1,000 condonation fee per semester.
- Strict Detention: Aggregate attendance below 65.0% results in automatic detention. Detained students cannot write Semester End Exams (SEE) and must repeat the semester in the next academic year.

## 3. Examination Grading Scale and Passing Marks
Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) carries 30% weightage, and Semester End Examination (SEE) carries 70% weightage.
- Passing Threshold: Minimum 40% marks in SEE (28 out of 70) AND 40% overall aggregate (40 out of 100).
- Grading Scale: O (90-100%, 10 pts), A+ (80-89%, 9 pts), A (70-79%, 8 pts), B+ (60-69%, 7 pts), B (50-59%, 6 pts), C (40-49%, 5 pts), F (<40%, 0 pts).

## 4. Revaluation and Challenge Evaluation
Students dissatisfied with SEE evaluation can apply for revaluation within 10 days of results declaration:
- Revaluation Fee: INR 500 per subject.
- Challenge Valuation: INR 2,000 per subject (fee refunded if marks increase by >15%).
