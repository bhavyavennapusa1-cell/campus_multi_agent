# Student Grievance Redressal Procedure & SLA Matrix

## 1. Academic & Marks Evaluation Grievances
Appeals regarding mid-term CIE evaluation, attendance recording disputes, or faculty misconduct.
- SLA Resolution Window: 3 working days.
- Escalation Flow: Subject Teacher $\rightarrow$ Class Mentor $\rightarrow$ Head of Department (HOD) $\rightarrow$ Dean Academics.

## 2. Hostel & Facilities Maintenance Complaints
Grievance tickets related to room repairs, mess hygiene, water supply, or curfew extensions.
- SLA Resolution Window: 2 working days (emergency plumbing/electrical within 4 hours).
- Escalation Flow: Resident Warden $\rightarrow$ Chief Warden $\rightarrow$ Campus Estate Officer.

## 3. IT Helpdesk & Network Infrastructure Issues
Network connectivity issues, student portal login failures, or lab workstation hardware bugs.
- SLA Resolution Window: 1 working day (24 hours).
- Escalation Flow: IT Helpdesk Ticket System $\rightarrow$ Network Administrator $\rightarrow$ Head of Computer Center.

## 4. Formal Grievance Escalation & Appeal Committee
If a grievance ticket remains unresolved past SLA limits, students can file a formal appeal to the Ombudsperson Committee via `grievance.committee@vasavi.ac.in`. The committee meets every Thursday at 03:00 PM.
