# Hostel rules

Hostel gates close at 9:30 PM on weekdays and 11:00 PM on weekends. Students
returning after closing time must sign in at the warden's office.

Guests are not permitted in hostel rooms after 8:00 PM. Day visitors must
register at the reception and carry a visitor pass at all times.

Room maintenance requests should be submitted through the hostel portal or
directly to the floor warden, and are typically resolved within 48 hours.
