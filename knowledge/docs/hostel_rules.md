# Residence Hall and Hostel Regulations

## 1. Curfew Timings and Gate Control
- Weekdays (Monday to Friday): Hostel main entry gate closes strictly at 22:30 (10:30 PM IST).
- Weekends (Saturday & Sunday): Entry gate closes at 23:30 (11:30 PM IST).
- Late Entry Penalty Framework:
  - 1st Offense: Written warning logged + parent/guardian automated alert.
  - 2nd Offense: Financial penalty of ₹500.
  - 3rd Offense: 7-day hostel suspension, mandatory parental meeting with Chief Warden, and INR 500 fine.

## 2. Leave Application and Outpass Protocol
- Advance Application: Outpass requests for overnight stay or home visits must be logged through the Student Hostel Portal at least 24 to 48 hours prior to departure.
- Parental Verification: Outpass approvals require parental authorization via automated IVRS or SMS response.

## 3. Mess Operations & Rebate Rules
- Mess Schedule: Breakfast (07:30-09:00), Lunch (12:15-14:00), Evening Snacks (16:30-18:00), Dinner (19:30-21:15).
- Mess Rebate: Residents absent for a minimum of 5 continuous calendar days are eligible for a mess fee rebate of ₹75 per day (notice required 3 days prior).

## 4. Room Allotment & Visitor Rules
- Room Allotment: 1st & 2nd-year students are allotted triple-sharing rooms; 3rd & 4th-year students are allotted double-sharing rooms prioritized by CGPA.
- Visitor Access: Visitors (parents/guardians only) are permitted strictly in the Visitors Lounge between 16:00 and 19:00 IST.
- Day scholars, friends, and overnight guests are strictly prohibited inside resident rooms (INR 2,000 fine per instance).
